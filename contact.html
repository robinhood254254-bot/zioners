COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you enquire.

Here are a few practical details to help you plan. If you have another question, include it in your message.

Ask us directly ↗
What kind of events do you play?+

We play church and worship gatherings, weddings and celebrations, company events, concerts, festivals and community events. Tell us what you’re planning and we’ll see if it’s a fit.

Where are you based? Can you travel?+

We’re based in Mwea and can travel for events around Kenya. Share the venue or town in your enquiry so we can discuss logistics.

Can you tailor the music to our occasion?+

Yes. We’ll talk through the mood, audience, schedule and any special requests before recommending a set and setup.

What do you need from the venue?+

It depends on the band size and venue. Once we know your event details, we can discuss space, power, sound equipment and setup time.

How do we check availability or get a quote?+

Send us your event date, location and a few details using the enquiry form. We’ll follow up to discuss availability and a suitable quote.
