PRAISE BANQUET / EVENT REFLECTION

A room full of praise: looking back at the Mega Praise Banquet

The Mega Praise Banquet brings worship, music and community into one shared gathering. These photographs capture the people at the centre of the event: the worshippers, musicians and guests who made the evening their own.

For The Zioners, events like this are a reminder that live music is something we make together. The band prepares the sound, but the congregation brings its own voice and energy to the room.

FROM THE ARCHIVE / 1 OCTOBER 2023

Remembering the October 2023 Mega Praise Banquet

The archive includes photographs from the Mega Praise Banquet held on 1 October 2023, featuring Pastor Dennis, Dr Ipyana and the wider worship community.

Looking back through these images gives us a way to honour the people and shared moments that continue to shape The Zioners.

PEOPLE / THE BAND

The people behind every note

Vocalists and instrumentalists each bring their own part to the sound. The group photographs and live-session images in our gallery show the people who prepare, perform and serve together.

That shared work is at the heart of the organisation: a team joining its different gifts to help a gathering find its voice.
