OUR VISION / OUR MISSION

Worship that
draws us deeper.

The Zioners Band exists to strengthen worship through musical excellence and equip people to serve.

THE VISION

Step up the worship standard in places of worship, helping the body of Christ grow a deeper, stronger relationship with God.

THE MISSION

Offer exposure and training to young talents and teams, raising praise and worship standards in their stations of assignment.

THE ASSIGNMENT

Empower individuals and teams to serve with musical excellence and help people experience deeper moments of worship.
