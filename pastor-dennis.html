PASTOR DENNIS / MINISTRY & LEADERSHIP

Serving through
worship and mentorship.

Pastor Dennis brings over 20 years of ministry experience to Mwea People's Church (MPC), where he currently serves in leadership alongside his work with The Zioners Band.

His ministry journey has included service under the leadership of Apostle Dr. Francis Wanderi at the People's Church and Reverend Samuel Chomba at Kagumo People's Church. Those seasons of service have informed his approach to worship leadership and the development of teams.

In 2022, he was recognized as Worship Pastor of the Year by Miruri Awards. He is known for passionate leadership in creating inspiring worship experiences and connecting with diverse audiences.

His work includes worship leadership, team coordination and mentoring musicians. Through The Zioners Band, he leads an assignment focused on helping individuals and teams serve with greater musical excellence and purpose.

Pastor Dennis has also appeared in television and radio programmes, including The Perspective on Elevate TV.
